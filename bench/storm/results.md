**Columns.** *Sent (wire)*: request bodies as sent (the Agent zstd-compresses; the others send uncompressed). *Sent, gzip*: the same bodies gzipped by the collector, to compare at equal compression. *Backend writes*: BugBuster's fold upserts plus the example events a backend keeps (at most 6 per issue); one record per span for OpenTelemetry. *Errors delivered*: occurrences that reached the collector — as a span, or inside a fold's count. *App CPU added* and *Peak RSS added* are over the no-telemetry baseline running the same storm.
